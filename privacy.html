Privacy Policy

This policy outlines how 47 Cloud handles user data.

Data Collection

We collect only the email address and password required to create and maintain your account. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text.

File Storage

Files uploaded to the platform are stored in isolated directories tied to your user ID. We do not inspect, analyze, or share the contents of your files.

Third Parties

We do not use third-party analytics, tracking cookies, or advertising networks. Infrastructure is hosted via Supabase and Netlify, which are bound by their respective data processing agreements.

Data Deletion

You may delete your files at any time via the application interface. To request complete account deletion, contact the system administrator.
